# The thesis as submitted

`Duyst_Thesis.pdf` is the Master of Environmental Science thesis submitted to
the Yale School of the Environment, titled *Urban-Methane Transfers (2000 –
2018): A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) Approach at Forecasting Historical
XCH4 Emissions through Urban Boundaries and Paddied Rice Extents Along China's
YRD*, advised by Xuhui Lee. Its title page is dated April 28, 2023. The file
has not been altered in any way by this repository; it was copied in byte for
byte and its SHA-256 is recorded in the commit that added it.

One caveat about which version this is. The PDF's own metadata gives a creation
date of May 15, 2023 and a modification date of June 20, 2023, both later than
the April 28 date printed on the title page. That is consistent with the file
having been regenerated or revised after the date it carries, so a later
revision may exist that this copy is not. Nothing available here settles the
question, and no attempt has been made to guess: this is the file that was to
hand, verified unchanged, and the discrepancy is recorded rather than resolved.

`ERRATA.md` at the repository root documents defects found in this work during
the 2026 audit. One is worth naming here because it bears directly on how the
document reads. Figure 4.7, captioned as the CNN-forecasted historical XCH4
emissions for 2000 and 2010, does not show model predictions. Both of its
panels are the same embedded image, and that image is also Figure 4.5(a) on the
preceding page: the raw 2018 TROPOMI observation, carrying the title "Raw
TROPOMI XCH4 Concentrations (2018)" baked into the picture. Anyone reading the
results should read that section against the errata.
